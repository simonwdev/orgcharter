The chart you are looking at (the baseline or a scenario), in Miro's layout: titles under Name and names under Job Title.

Drop a saved workbook here, or the Miro org-chart CSV A workbook saved from this planner holds the chart, its scenarios and the skills together. A Miro export needs the columns PersonID, SupervisorID, Name, Job Title, Hiring, Location, plus an optional Notes column. Rows whose name is (TEAM) are treated as teams. Streams, roles and locations are inferred from the file. The skills file is optional: a CSV or Excel workbook with one row per person per skill and the columns PersonID, Skill, plus optional Skill Group and Detail. Nothing leaves your browser.
